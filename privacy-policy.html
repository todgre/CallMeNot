CallMeNot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

AppsStudio HQ ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CallMeNot mobile application. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled by the app.

Information We Collect

CallMeNot may access the following information on your device when you grant the relevant Android permissions and use the related features:

  • Contacts and phone numbers: Contact information may be read on-device for contact and whitelist features.
  • Call information: The app uses Android call-screening functionality and, where permission is granted, may read call-log information for app features. The app's local records are stored on your device.
  • App preferences and whitelist: These are stored locally on your device.

The app includes Firebase SDKs and a Firebase project configuration. The current app version has no sign-in flow and does not invoke its Firestore sync operations from the app, so cloud sync is not offered. A Firebase configuration alone does not mean your whitelist is uploaded. We cannot determine from the app whether data may have been stored by a prior version or through another service.

How We Use Your Information

  • To screen incoming calls based on your whitelist preferences
  • To provide contact, whitelist, and call-related features on your device

Data Storage

Local storage: App data such as your whitelist and preferences is stored locally on your device. Android's app-storage controls can remove app data; Android backup or device-transfer settings may preserve or restore some app preferences and whitelist data. This policy does not claim that the app's local database is encrypted.

Cloud storage: The current app version does not provide cloud sync or an in-app way to view or delete cloud data. Signing out of a Firebase session, if one exists, only ends that session; it does not delete data previously stored in Firebase.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. The app includes Firebase Authentication and Firestore SDKs and is configured for Firebase, but the current app version does not offer sign-in or invoke Firestore sync. Other third-party services may process information as described in their own terms:

  • Google Play: For subscription management and billing

Data Security

Data stored by the app is subject to the security of your device and Android. We do not make a separate claim here that app data is encrypted. Firebase services, if used outside the current app data flow, are subject to Google's terms and controls.

Your Rights

You can manage local app data and permissions using the app and Android settings. The current app version has no account creation, cloud-sync, or account-deletion flow. If you used an earlier version or otherwise believe your data may be in Firebase, contact us to ask about it; signing out or clearing this app's local storage does not delete cloud records.

  • Remove individual whitelist entries using the app's whitelist controls
  • Remove app-local data using Android's app storage controls
  • Ask us about a data request at privacy@appsstudiohq.com

Children's Privacy

CallMeNot is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy within the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.
