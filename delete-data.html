CallMeNot - Delete Your Data

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

This page describes deletion options for data stored locally by CallMeNot and explains the limits of cloud-data deletion. The current app version does not offer sign-in, cloud sync, or in-app account deletion.

Delete Whitelist Entries

Step 1: Open the CallMeNot app

Step 2: Go to the Whitelist tab

Step 3: Use the entry's delete control to remove it from the app's local whitelist.

Cloud Data and Account Deletion

The current app version does not provide cloud sync or an in-app way to view, delete, or close a Firebase account. If Settings shows an existing Firebase session, choosing Sign Out ends the session only. It does not delete local data, Firebase account records, or any cloud data that may have been stored by an earlier version or another service.

If you believe you previously used a version that stored information in Firebase, contact privacy@appsstudiohq.com to ask about the data and request deletion. We cannot confirm from this app whether such records exist or promise a deletion timeline on this page. A request is not confirmation that data has been found or deleted.

Delete All Local Data

To remove CallMeNot's app-local data from your device:

Step 1: Go to your device Settings

Step 2: Navigate to Apps > CallMeNot

Step 3: Tap Clear Data or Clear Storage

This removes app data stored locally by CallMeNot and may not remove call-history data maintained separately by Android or your carrier. Android backup or device transfer may restore app preferences or whitelist data later; review your device backup settings if needed. Clearing local storage does not delete Firebase or Google Play records.

Other Data

  • Firebase: The current app version cannot delete Firebase account or cloud records. Contact us if you believe you have data from a previous version; no deletion or retention period is guaranteed by this page.
  • Subscription: Subscription management is handled by Google Play. Visit Google Play Subscriptions to manage a subscription. Deleting app data does not cancel it.
